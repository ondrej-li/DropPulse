Why DropPulse?

High-demand concert tickets, theater plays, and movie screenings (like IMAX) frequently sell out within minutes. Yet reservations are continuously cancelled and seats quietly return to inventory at unpredictable hours. Constantly refreshing browser tabs throughout the day is tedious and unreliable. DropPulse acts as your automated availability sentry—monitoring public event pages around the clock and notifying you the second seats open up.

How DropPulse Works in 4 Steps

1

1. Submit Event URL

Enter the public URL of the ticket page, movie showing, or festival portal you want to monitor.

2. Background Checks

Key Capabilities & Design Principles

Automated Availability Tracking

DropPulse monitors publicly accessible event and ticket pages in the background, detecting released seats and cancellations without excessive requests.

Instant Email Alerts

The moment tickets or seats become available, DropPulse sends an immediate alert to your verified email with a direct link to the booking page.

Ethical & Human-First

DropPulse strictly respects target websites: we do not perform automated CAPTCHA solving. If a challenge is encountered, the check pauses so you can verify manually.

Privacy & Minimal Data

Sign-in is used solely to authenticate your account and route alert emails. We use no ad trackers, no analytics pixels, and never sell user data.

Application Purpose & Google API Terms of Service Compliance

The DropPulse application is dedicated to automated event ticket availability tracking and notification delivery. Google Sign-In is used exclusively to authenticate authorized users and deliver email notifications via minimal OAuth scopes (openid, email, profile).

DropPulse's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Prohibition on AI-Generated Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery (AI NCII): In strict compliance with the Google APIs Terms of Service, DropPulse does not use Google APIs, user data, or artificial intelligence systems to generate, create, process, host, or distribute Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery (AI NCII), sexually explicit material, or harmful content.
