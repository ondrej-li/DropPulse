Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026 · Version 1.0

This is an invitation-only private preview. Sign-in is limited to accounts on the operator's allowlist, and there is no public registration.

DropPulse watches a ticket page and tells you when something becomes available. This policy explains what personal data we process, why we process it, who receives it, and the rights you have. It applies to dp.ondralinek.cz, the DropPulse app and the notification emails we send.

This policy is available in Czech and English. The Czech version is authoritative; the English translation is provided for convenience.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of the personal data described here is Ondřej Linek, an individual based in the Czech Republic. Contact: me@ondralinek.cz.

The service is hosted on a server operated by Hetzner Online GmbH in Falkenstein, Germany (EU). Availability notifications are sent through the mail server on the same host; we do not use a third-party email delivery service.

2. Invitation-only access

Access is by invitation. Only email addresses on the allowlist maintained by the operator can sign in. There is no public registration. If your address is not on the allowlist, sign-in is refused and no account is created. The allowlist currently contains only the operator's own address.

We check the email address and the verification status returned by your sign-in provider. We do not use the allowlist for advertising or profiling.

3. What we collect

Account and sign-in data

  • your email address and whether the provider marked it as verified;
  • a display name if the provider supplies one. Google supplies a name. Apple sends a name only during the first sign-in, and the current version of the service does not store it;
  • a profile-picture URL if Google supplies one;
  • the provider name and the provider's stable account identifier (not a password);
  • account timestamps: created, updated and last sign-in.

We never receive or store your password for Google, Apple or any other provider. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we receive the Apple relay address, not your personal address; notifications go to that relay.

Watch data

  • the name, URL, check interval and enabled state of each watch;
  • the last check time, HTTP status, error text, availability state, notification state and the last notification time or error.

Session and security data

  • an opaque session identifier (we store a hash, not the token), the browser user agent, IP address, session times and revocation state;
  • a hash of the CSRF token;
  • OAuth flow data: hashed state and browser-binding values, the nonce, the PKCE verifier, the redirect URI, the return path and the expiry;
  • server logs: IP address, request path, status, timing and a request ID.

Email

Availability notifications go to the verified address on your account. The mail server processes the message and keeps delivery logs under its own rotation policy.

We do not collect passwords, payment-card data (billing is not active), special-category data, precise location, contacts or advertising identifiers. We do not use analytics or tracking pixels.

4. Why we process it and on what legal basis

  • Creating and authenticating your account, keeping you signed in and providing the watch service, including notifications: performance of a contract with you (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Limiting access to invited accounts and preventing abuse: our legitimate interest in operating a closed preview and protecting the service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Securing the service, investigating errors and attacks, and keeping logs: our legitimate interest in security (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Complying with legal obligations, such as responding to lawful requests: legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Optional name and profile picture: you choose what your provider shares; we process it to display your account (contract, or your consent expressed through the provider).

We do not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects and do not build advertising profiles. We send only the availability notifications you configure; we do not send marketing email.

5. Cookies and local storage

Strictly necessary cookies and storage:

  • __Host-dp_session keeps you signed in. It is HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Lax, valid for up to 30 days; the server-side session ends after 30 days of inactivity or 90 days after sign-in.
  • __Host-dp_csrf protects against cross-site request forgery. It is not HttpOnly so the app can read it and send it as a header. It is Secure, SameSite=Lax and has the same lifetime as the session.
  • __Host-dp_oauth binds a sign-in flow to your browser. It is HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=None (Apple posts its callback cross-site), and lasts about 10 minutes.
  • localStorage key droppulse-theme remembers your light or dark theme choice. It stays until you clear it and is never sent to us.

We use no advertising, analytics or cross-site tracking cookies. The public landing and privacy pages set no cookies.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser. Blocking the session cookie means you cannot sign in.

6. Who receives your data

  • Google (Google Ireland Limited) and Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd.) when you choose to sign in. They authenticate you and return the data described above. Their own privacy policies apply: Google, Apple.
  • Hetzner Online GmbH, our hosting provider in Germany (EU), which stores the database and runs the server.
  • The mail server on the same host, for notification delivery.
  • Public authorities or courts where the law requires it.

We do not sell, rent or share personal data for advertising. We use no analytics, ad networks or data brokers.

If we add AI-assisted features, we will update this policy and tell you before sending page content to an AI provider. No such features are active today.

7. International transfers

The service and database are hosted in the EU (Germany). Google and Apple may process data outside the EU/EEA under their own safeguards, such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses; their policies describe this. We do not transfer data to other third countries on our own initiative.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account data: for as long as your account exists. After deletion we delete or anonymise the account records, except what we must keep by law.
  • Sessions: 30 days of inactivity, and no longer than 90 days after sign-in; expired records are removed.
  • OAuth flows: about 10 minutes, then purged.
  • Watches and their last check and notification state: until you delete them or your account is deleted.
  • Server and mail logs: for the limited period needed for security and troubleshooting; logs rotate and are not kept indefinitely.
  • Records needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims: for the applicable limitation period.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data, to have it rectified or erased, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise these rights, email me@ondralinek.cz. We respond within one month; for complex requests we may extend that by two months and will tell you.

You may also complain to the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ), Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Prague 7, Czech Republic, www.uoou.cz, or to the supervisory authority in the EU country where you live or work.

To delete your account, email us from the account address. We will revoke your sessions and delete the account and its watches. Some data may be retained where the law requires it.

10. Security

All traffic uses TLS and HSTS. Sessions are server-side and their tokens are stored hashed. Cookies are HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite where appropriate. The service uses CSRF protection, rate limits, access controls and a least-privilege service account, and stores no passwords. No system is perfectly secure; if you find a problem, email me@ondralinek.cz. If a personal-data breach occurs, we will notify the supervisory authority and affected users where the law requires it.

11. Children

The service is not intended for children under 15. We do not knowingly collect their personal data. If we learn that we have, we will delete it. Contact us if you believe a child has signed in.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the version and date and, for material changes, notify you on the site or by email where appropriate. The current version is always available at this address.

13. Contact
